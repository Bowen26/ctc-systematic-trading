How the click list decides

Bot quotes
Bid = floor(fair − 1.00), ask = ceil(fair + 1.00), at the stale spot and IV; 3 levels of 200, $1.00 apart. No bid when fair is under 1.00. Window A uses the stale spot; Window B uses the new spot with the stale IV.
Edge per lot
New fair minus the level price (buys) or level price minus new fair (sells). The half-spread is already inside the level price.
Orders and limits
Fills happen at the resting order's price (confirmed on Slack), so each line is one order priced at the deepest level it needs: it fills the better levels first, at their own prices. Each option has a 300 position limit, which also caps order size, so room to buy is 300 − position and room to sell is 300 + position. Press Filled · add to positions once the orders fill, or type the positions from the exchange's Positions panel.
Round 1
Take a level when its edge beats the buffer: 0.10 on the deep in-the-money lines (lowest-strike call, highest-strike put), 0.30 on the rest until T is confirmed.
Rounds 2–3
Pick one: each analyst is a candidate weighted by p; take a level when expected edge beats 0.25 per lot (at E4–E5 two of three must also be positive). Combine: bias-corrected, inverse-variance blend with one outlier dropped; take a level when edge beats ½ × vega × τ. All three must agree: the edge must be positive under every analyst (E4, and E7 until the formula is known).
Vol-free
Fair at the new spot and the stale IV minus the stale mid: the part of the edge that does not depend on getting the IV right. In Round 2 take it at full size.
Never
Market orders, resting orders across T+0 or T+60, or quotes before the snap in Rounds 2–3. CANCEL ALL at T+145.
