What the numbers mean

m̂ (m-hat)
Points the average human is expected to score per race, given every race so far. Before Race 1 it is 8.4, about a 5th–6th place finish. 6.8 means the humans are no better than the CPUs; 10.5 means three of the four usually finish in the top 4.
mi
The same thing for one human. Stronger humans sit above m̂. They drive the score contracts: #1 is roughly the leader's score + races left × the leader's mi + a premium for "best of four".
Race-win %
Chance this human is the best-placed human in a future race, so the fair value of their race-winner contract. It is the same for every future race.
Series winner
Fair value of the series-winner contract: pays 100 for finishing top alone, 50 to each human tied for the lead.
#1 · #2 · Top-2
Expected final series total of the best human, of the second best, and their sum. sd is the uncertainty; 5–95% covers nine outcomes in ten. Each point is 1,000 on a full position.
Quote
Fair value ± the playbook's half-spread for this stage: wide before Race 1, tighter as the series goes on.
Hard bounds
Values the contract cannot settle outside, whatever happens. A price outside them is free money.
